- (decision) Working mode: DESIGN HANDOFF. Discovery is assembled into a producer-shaped prompt for Google Stitch; Dre runs it externally and saves outputs into this workspace. EXPERIENCE.md follows via a later Update pass.
- (decision) Form factor: mobile-only single column, centered in the Telegram Desktop viewport with the Paper canvas filling the remainder. Desktop is a TEST target, not a design target. No adaptive wide layout, no desktop hard-block.
- (decision) Demo setup is multiple phones side by side. Realtime is the centerpiece: item rows need visible arrival motion, participant avatars pop in on claim, and the sticky footer counter must visibly tick. Live claim presence is PROMOTED out of P1 into the designed surface.
- (decision) Stitch handoff covers ALL 12 IA screens plus the All-square card as 9b. Dre chose full coverage after being told Stitch quality degrades on long prompts; mitigation is a prompt structured in three runnable waves rather than a reduced scope.
- (decision) Settle climax is two-tier: individual payments settle QUIETLY IN PLACE (amount row swaps to Settled Mint, check draws in, progress ring advances, no takeover). The celebration card fires ONCE, only when the final obligation confirms and the group reaches all square. Prevents: spending the emotional peak on every tap.
- (decision) All-square card composition: apricot wash, single check, 'All square', bill title and total, 'n of n settled', the success line 'Your group tab. Settled.', and one Share-to-group action.
- (assumption) Demo dataset is fixed across every screen for consistency: group 'Sukhumvit Dinner', total 1,840 THB, 5 people - Maya (organizer, recipient), Andre (the viewer, owes 291.73 THB), Noi, Ploy, Tim. Matches Brief section 5.1 and the ledger example in section 11.6.
- (event) Stitch handoff prompt assembled at stitch-handoff-prompt.md. Structured as Section A (design system, restated per wave) plus three waves: W1 Tabs/Claim Board/Payment Sheet, W2 Bill Review/Payment Progress/All Square/Tip Composer/Group, W3 Launch/New Tab/Receipt Review/Activity/You.
- (decision) Claim Board specifies four item-row states explicitly so the visual language is unambiguous: unclaimed-needs-owner (amber left accent), claimed-by-viewer (soft blue row tint, 'Yours'), shared (overlapping avatars plus per-head caption), claimed-by-other (quiet name label, no tint).
- (decision) Live presence rendered in the Claim Board header as an overlapping avatar stack with a pulsing dot ('3 people are here right now'), following the multiple-phones demo decision.
- (decision) Payment Sheet hides all technical detail behind ONE collapsed disclosure row labelled 'Route, fees and network'. Quote freshness shows as a countdown, not a technical expiry timestamp.
- (decision) Payment Progress is a four-step vertical stepper (Approved in your wallet / Verifying / Sending to Maya / Confirmed), not a spinner. A failed variant surfaces 'Quote expired. Refresh it.' with Try again and Back to tab. Raw error codes are never shown.
- (decision) The wallet address appears in exactly ONE place in the whole product - the You screen, truncated and muted. The explorer link appears in exactly one place - an expanded Activity row. These are the only two acknowledgements of blockchain in the UI.
- (decision) Receipt Review reads as a corrected document, not a model output: amber discrepancy card at top, amber outlines on low-confidence rows, inline editing everywhere, no confidence percentages, no sparkle or robot iconography.
- (decision) Launch/Auth has zero buttons and zero login affordances - wordmark, progress indicator, and 'Getting your tab ready...' only. Enforces the seamless-auth promise visually.
- (constraint) You screen must not become a crypto dashboard: no token balances, no portfolio value, no charts, no network selector.

- (decision) DESIGN.md written as the token contract rather than left as a Stitch stub. Reason: EXPERIENCE.md is spec'd to reference DESIGN.md tokens by {name}, and /design needs a resolvable token contract to render against. Content is a TRANSCRIPTION of Dre's Brief section 9 palette/type/layout decisions into the Google Labs DESIGN.md shape - not new design authorship. Open to override.
- (decision) Claiming is ADDITIVE, not exclusive. Two people tapping the same item both get it and the row becomes a shared split - no conflict dialog, no rejection, no loser. This removes conflict resolution from the interface entirely and matches what a real table does.
- (decision) Rounding asymmetry from largest-remainder allocation is DISCLOSED as its own breakdown line ('Rounding +B0.01'), never silent. Silent asymmetry between people who ordered identical items is the fastest way to lose a group's trust.
- (decision) Bill Review is organizer-only. Participants get their own expanded breakdown inside the Payment Sheet instead. Flagged as [NOTE FOR UX] for Dre to confirm.
- (decision) Pull-to-refresh is BANNED product-wide. Convex is reactive; a refresh gesture implies data might be stale, which contradicts the product's core claim.
- (decision) Long-press is unbound everywhere. Only exception is the hidden demo-mode 'Use sample receipt' affordance.
- (decision) Motion budget is exactly three sanctioned animations: avatar arriving in a stack (200ms), check drawing on confirmation (400ms), all-square wash (600ms, once per bill). Haptics limited to three moments: claim, lock, confirmed payment.
- (decision) The Telegram bot's messages are treated as a DESIGNED SURFACE of this product, specified in EXPERIENCE.md, not as an external channel. One status message per tab, edited in place, five sanctioned events, never individual debts.
- (decision) A cancelled wallet prompt is explicitly NOT a failure state - it returns to the Payment Sheet unchanged with no message. Cancelling a signature is a normal human act.
- (decision) Semantic color never travels alone: every owed/settled/warning state also carries a word or glyph, so the product is settleable without color discrimination.

- (change) Dre approved the direction and layout, and asked for a premium pass on color and typography only - Stripe / QuickBooks / TurboTax register. Layout, IA, copy, and interaction untouched.
- (override) Palette reworked from warm-craft to cool-premium. Canvas F7F5EF->F4F7FA, ink 171714->0A2038 (deep navy, never black - the single highest-leverage substitution), muted 6C6B63->55677D slate, border E7E3D8->DFE7EF cool hairline, primary 2F66F6->1E51D2 deeper blue, settled 147A55->0B7561 emerald, owed C94552->B32B44 crimson, warning 8A5A00->9A6209 amber, tip FF8A5B->A85F2E burnished terracotta. Principle: neutrals cool, accents deep, saturation spent only on meaning.
- (decision) Typeface changed from Figtree to Instrument Sans. Figtree is rounded and friendly, which read consumer rather than financial. Instrument Sans is a modern grotesque with tight apertures; body carries -0.006em tracking and display sizes tighten as they grow (-0.032em at 42px).
- (override) Reversed DESIGN.md's own 'no all-caps labels' rule. Section headings are now micro-labels: 11px/600/0.07em uppercase in ink-subtle. A quiet letterspaced label above a group of figures is a deliberate borrowing from financial software and signals structure without competing.
- (override) Elevation rule amended from pure border-led to border-led plus one hairline of lift: cards carry 0 1px 2px rgba(10,32,56,0.045); primary buttons carry inset 0 -1px 0 rgba(10,32,56,0.24). Threshold: removing the lift should make the page look cheaper, not obviously different.
- (decision) Avatar tints promoted into DESIGN.md as tokens avatar-1..5 (terracotta, blue, emerald, amber, slate) at matched lightness, closing the gap flagged at first handover.
- (event) Every text pairing contrast-checked against WCAG AA at its real size before applying. Two values failed and were darkened rather than shipped: ink-subtle 8496A8->61748B and tip C0703C->A85F2E. Final set: 0 failures across 21 pairings.
